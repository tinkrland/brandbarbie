## what's in the book

five sections, one job each:

1. **strategy** — audience, archetypes, personality, moodboards. the thinking.
2. **visual identity** — logos, palettes, type, illustration, photography, layout. the seeing.
3. **applications** — social, print, packaging, website, email. the doing.
4. **vocabulary** — glossary, voice, positioning, social copy. the saying.
5. **brand kit** — what to grab when you need the brand in a hurry. the shipping.

every page links to its neighbors — hover any link to preview it before you click.
