the body face carries almost every word the brand prints: warm, plain, quietly confident, built to disappear into the reading.

:::tip
starter set: this page documents the role. swap in the final typeface, keep the rules.
:::

## its job

- everything that isn't a headline: body copy, lists, captions, buttons, forms
- long headlines too: more than 7 words drops to body, at display scale if needed

## the rules

- regular and one bold; no other weights needed in print
- never centered: centering is for headlines and wedding invitations
- generous leading for print, tighter for screen: see [[website typography]]
- the sans must look comfortable next to the serif's roundness: sharp-cornered grotesques need not apply

## pairing

its contrast with the [[display face]] is the whole system: soft serif speaks, warm sans explains. the pairing rules live in [[brand typography]].
