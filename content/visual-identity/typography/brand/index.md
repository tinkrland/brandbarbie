type is the brand's voice made visible. two typefaces, no freelancing.

## the pairing (starter set: replace with final fonts)

- **display: a soft round-terminal serif**. headlines, pull quotes, [[brand board]] labels only
- **body: a warm readable sans**. everything else

## rules

- display for headlines of 7 words or fewer; longer headlines go body-size, not display
- never center body copy; centering is for headlines and wedding invitations
- one weight per rung of [[type hierarchy]]
- no italics on the display face: its personality doesn't need them

:::tip
web use of both faces is defined separately in [[website typography]]: different mediums, different rules.
:::
