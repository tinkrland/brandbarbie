the display face is the brand's speaking voice: soft, round terminals, a little romantic, never casual about it.

:::tip
starter set: this page documents the role. swap in the final typeface, keep the rules.
:::

## its job

- headlines of 7 words or fewer
- [[brand board]] labels, cover type, pull quotes
- nothing else: display type on body copy is shouting

## the rules

- no italics: its personality doesn't need them
- no all-caps runs longer than three words
- set tight but never touching; spacing follows the [[type hierarchy]]
- one weight per composition

## pairing

it only ever appears next to the [[body face]]: why the pairing works is documented in [[brand typography]].
