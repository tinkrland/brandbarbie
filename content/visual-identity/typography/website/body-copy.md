web body copy is read on phones, in sunlight, on a train. it gets the most conservative rules in the book.

## the numbers

- body minimum {hl:yellow|16px}: no exceptions, no 14px asterisks
- line-height 1.6 for paragraphs, 1.2 for headings
- measure: 60 to 75 characters per line; wider is a layout bug, not a design choice
- paragraph spacing beats indents on the web: space between, no first-line indent

## the rules

- links are always underlined, brand pink: affordance beats aesthetic
- never justify text on the web: the rivers are unspeakable
- captions and meta may drop to 13px minimum, in the mono face
