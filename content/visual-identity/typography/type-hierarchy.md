the hierarchy is a ladder with even spacing. every piece of text sits on a rung.

## the ladder (1.250 scale — starter values)

| rung | desktop | mobile | use |
|---|---|---|---|
| h1 | 56px | 40px | one per page |
| h2 | 44px | 32px | section heads |
| h3 | 35px | 28px | sub-sections |
| h4 | 28px | 24px | card titles |
| body | 18px | 16px | paragraphs |
| small | 14px | 14px | meta, captions |
| label | 12px | 12px | chips, eyebrows |

## spacing law

- heading margins: space above is 2× space below — text hangs from its heading
- one h1 per page; if you need two, you need two pages

:::note
labels are uppercase-tracked or lowercase-mono, never both on the same surface.
:::
