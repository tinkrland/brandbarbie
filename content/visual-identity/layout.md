layout is the brand's posture. the stance: **generous whitespace, centered axis, playful edges**.

## the grid

- 12 columns web, 6 on mobile; 24px gutters
- outer margin minimum: 24px web, 16px mobile — content never touches the glass
- print: 10mm minimum margin on all sides

## composition habits

- hero: centered or left-third; never both in one system
- cards in rows of three or four (titles per [[type hierarchy]])
- one moment of play per layout — a rotated sticker, an arch mask (see [[graphic elements]]) — and nothing else competes with it

## the squint test

squint at any layout: you should see three values — headline, image, space. if you see a busy surface, the layout is overworked. remove, don't shrink.
