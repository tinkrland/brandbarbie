## usage notes

- noir backgrounds always take the mono-white logo (see [[backgrounds]])
- bubblegum is a punctuation here, not a paint — links, underlines, one glowing accent
- long-form text on noir needs the lighter line-height; test with [[contrast & accessibility]]
