the neutrals exist so the loud colors have somewhere to rest.

## swatches (starter set)

| role | name | hex |
|---|---|---|
| paper | warm paper | `#FAF3EC` |
| mid | greige | `#E8DED4` |
| deep | umber | `#5C4F47` |

## usage notes

- these pair with any palette — they're structure, not statement
- greige is the divider and card background of the system
- umber is for secondary text only on paper backgrounds; check [[contrast & accessibility]] before going lighter
