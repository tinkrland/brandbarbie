a palette is a cast; the ratio is the script. every composition splits 60/30/10, no exceptions and no eyeballing.

## the split

- **60: the background**. cream, or the active palette's field color (see [[palette library]]). space is not empty; it's the majority color doing its job
- **30: the secondary**. rose or a supporting tone: headers, shapes, blocks, cards
- **10: the accent**. bubblegum, butter, the sparkles. the garnish, never the meal

## reading a composition

ask three questions, in order:

1. what covers about 60%? if the answer is pink, the ratio is upside down
2. does the eye land where it should? the 10% is the arrow, not the wallpaper
3. would it survive in grayscale? value does the pointing, hue does the charming

## the carousels

ratios are taught as carousels: one composition, three slides, hex chips along the bottom.

- slide 1: the composition at rest
- slide 2: the same composition with the color areas outlined and labeled 60 / 30 / 10
- slide 3: the grayscale proof: value still points the eye without any hue

:::tip
ratio violations are the most common rookie mistake in [[brand board]] mockups: everything pink, nothing breathing.
:::
