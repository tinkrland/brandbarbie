illustration is where the brand gets to move. the style: **flat shapes, visible hand**.

## the style in five rules

1. shapes are flat and simplified — no gradients, no 3d
2. line weight is consistent and slightly wobbly — hand-drawn over vector-perfect
3. color comes from the active palette only; illustration is not a color loophole
4. one texture pass on everything (see [[textures]]) — grain is the house skin
5. one focal element per illustration; everything else is scenery

## subjects

the brand illustrates its world: [[motifs]], [[patterns]], and occasionally [[characters]]. products and people stay photographic (see [[photography]]).
