the grid pattern is discipline on display: a single motif, tiled flat, for places where the [[scatter pattern]] would be too much fun.

## construction

- one motif per pattern: mixing motifs in a grid reads as wallpaper from a catalog
- quarter or half-drop tiling, edge-to-edge: the seam should be invisible
- straight rotation: in a grid, everything sits upright, the jaunty angle is off duty

## where it goes

- linings and secondary packaging: the inside of the box, the back of the card
- tape and tissue: the supporting cast of the [[scatter pattern]]'s lead
- web backgrounds behind interactive areas: flat, calm, never competing with type

## rules

- grids earn calm: they never carry the accent color at full strength
- opacity caps at 15% on web, full strength in print only
