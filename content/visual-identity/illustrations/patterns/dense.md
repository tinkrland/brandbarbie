dense is pattern behaving like paint: motifs packed until the field reads as one saturated block, used exactly like a solid color.

## construction

- motifs touching, overlapping, no field showing through: the negative space becomes texture, not background
- two colors maximum: motif color and field color
- flat render: no gloss, no gradients, the density is the drama

## where it goes

- tapes and endpapers: the signature loud moment of the packaging system
- section dividers in decks and lookbooks: a dense band as a chapter break
- one dense element per composition: two is a quilt

## rules

- dense pattern never sits behind body copy: pattern is a surface, not a backdrop
- the 60/30/10 discipline still applies: a dense block counts as its color, see [[color ratios]]
