scatter is motifs at play: the [[motifs]] tossed across a field, random in rotation, even in spacing, loose in feel and strict in math underneath.

## construction

- motifs at 2 to 4 sizes within one field, weighted toward the small
- random rotation: nothing sits straight, nothing sits perpendicular to the field edge
- even spacing despite the randomness: no clumps, no bald spots, check with a squint

## where it goes

- wrapping paper and packaging interiors: the hero use
- endpapers, tape, the inside of a thank-you card
- web hero backgrounds at 4-6% opacity, behind nothing: see [[patterns]] for the surface rule

## scale

- print scatter is 2 to 3 times the scale of web scatter: the eye reads paper and pixels at different distances
