characters are a big commitment: a face that can't be undone. current stance: **no recurring character**. motifs (see [[motifs]]) carry the personality instead.

## if one is ever introduced

- one character, one name, one canon: a character with variants is a mascot franchise
- drawn in the [[illustration style]]: flat, wobbly line, grain pass
- never used in body copy; characters speak through placement, not speech bubbles
- retired gracefully: a character that lingers past its era becomes the brand's embarrassing tattoo
