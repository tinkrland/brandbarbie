current stance: **no recurring character**. the [[motifs]] carry the personality. but if the day comes, the rules are already written, and they are strict.

## the one, the only

- one character, one name, one canon: a character with variants is a mascot franchise, and franchises age in dog years
- the name is approved like a logo: spelled, capitalized, and never nicknamed in copy
- the design lives in the [[illustration style]]: flat, wobbly line, grain pass

## the conduct rules

- characters don't speak in body copy: no speech bubbles, no captions in their voice
- they gesture, react, and point: placement is their dialogue
- they never carry the logo: the character wears the brand, the brand doesn't wear the character

## the retirement plan

- a character gets a run of one to two years, then a decision: renew or retire
- retirement is announced and clean: a character that lingers past its era becomes the brand's embarrassing tattoo
- everything above is reread before the first sketch: these rules were cheap to write and expensive to ignore
