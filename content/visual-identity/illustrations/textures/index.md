texture is the difference between flat and lifeless. three official treatments:

## the set

- **grain**: 8-12% noise overlay on illustrations and photos; the default skin
- **gloss**: hard highlights on candy-like shapes; used sparingly, mostly on motifs (see [[motifs]])
- **risograph feel**: slight misregistration and overprint on patterns (see [[patterns]]); the nostalgia play

## rules

- one texture per composition: grain + gloss + riso is a craft fair
- texture is applied at export, not baked into source vectors
- on web, grain is a fixed overlay at 4% so it doesn't shimmer on scroll
