grain is the brand's default skin: a light noise pass that makes flat color feel printed instead of rendered.

## the recipe

- 8-12% monochrome noise over illustrations and photos: enough to soften, not enough to dirty
- gaussian, not uniform: uniform noise reads as a broken screen
- one grain size per project: mixed grain sizes read as an accident

## where it goes

- every flat illustration, at export: see [[illustration style]]
- photo overlays for the [[photography]] presets
- never on type, never on logos: the logo system stays clean, see [[logo misuse]]

## on the web

- grain is a fixed overlay at 4%: fixed, so it doesn't shimmer on scroll
- the overlay is one tiled png, not per-image noise: performance is a texture too

see [[textures]] for the other two treatments.
