the risograph feel is borrowed imperfection: the slight misregistration and overprint of riso printing, faked digitally, for warmth.

## the recipe

- two or three spot colors, multiply blended: overprint is the look, transparency sliders are not
- 2-4px misregistration between the color layers: off, but only just
- the same [[grain]] pass on top: riso without grain is a poster from a template

## where it goes

- patterns: the riso treatment's home, see [[patterns]]
- gig-style posters and event graphics: the nostalgia context
- never on the logo, never on packaging: those must print exactly, see [[logo misuse]]

## rules

- riso is a mood, not a default: one riso composition per project, maximum
- the misregistration must look like a machine did it, not a hand: random offsets read as sloppy, consistent ones read as riso
