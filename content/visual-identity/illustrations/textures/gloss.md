gloss is the candy jar of the texture set: hard-edged white highlights that make shapes look lickable. it is used sparingly, mostly on the [[motifs]].

## the recipe

- hard-edged white or cream highlight, 100% opacity, no blur: gloss is a shape, not a filter
- placed at the upper-left of the shape, following one light source for the whole composition
- one highlight per shape: two reads like a 2000s web button

## where it goes

- motifs: the [[the bow|bow]]'s loops, hearts, candy-like shapes
- product moments: a glossy sticker, a shine on the packaging mockup
- never on type, never on photos: gloss is illustration-only

## rules

- gloss is the strongest texture in the set: it doesn't combine with [[risograph]] or heavy [[grain]], one texture per composition
