paper texture is the ground the other treatments stand on: the faint tooth of a nice stock, felt before it's seen.

## the recipe

- subtle directional tooth, horizontal: like laid paper, not like sandpaper
- 3-6% strength as a background layer under illustrations and cards
- warm-tinted, matching the cream: cool gray paper makes the whole palette look sad

## where it goes

- full-page backgrounds in decks and lookbooks: the page itself is a material
- card and label backgrounds: the difference between a card and a rectangle
- in print, actual paper does this for free: don't double-apply

## rules

- paper is a base, not an accent: it never carries color or draws attention
- pairs with [[grain]] (they share the monochrome family), never with [[gloss]]
