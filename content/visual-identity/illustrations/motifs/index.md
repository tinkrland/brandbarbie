motifs are the brand's recurring symbols — the shapes that keep showing up until people associate them with us.

## the starter set

- **the bow** — the signature. bows are big, small, everywhere; the bow is brandbarbie's period at the end of the sentence
- **hearts** — rounded, slightly asymmetric, never the emoji heart
- **stars** — four-point sparkle, always at a jaunty angle
- **sugar dots** — tiny polka-dot clusters used as fill and confetti

## usage rules

- motifs are accents, not wallpaper — see [[graphic elements]] for placement
- they may be filled, outlined, or cut out of a shape
- never stretch a motif to fill space; repeat it instead
