sugar dots are the sprinkle finish: tiny circles in clusters, doing the work of texture without the weight of pattern.

## anatomy

- dots 2 to 4px at illustration scale, round, evenly sized within a cluster
- clustered loose: three to seven dots, never a perfect grid (the grid is a [[patterns|pattern]]'s job)
- sized in a rhythm: one large, two medium, a few small

## usage

- trailing off the [[the bow|bow]] or [[stars]]: the motion lines of joy
- filling negative space in collages without adding weight
- borders and dividers: a dotted trail instead of a rule, see [[graphic elements]]

## rules

- sugar dots never sit on top of type: they are confetti, not interference
- one cluster logic per composition: scattered sugar everywhere is a snow globe
