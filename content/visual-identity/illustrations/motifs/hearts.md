the heart is the warm half of the motif vocabulary: affection with taste, and a firm ban on the emoji version.

## anatomy

- rounded lobes, soft point: nothing sharp at the bottom
- slightly asymmetric: leans a degree or two, like it's listening
- drawn, not typed: the difference between a motif and a keyboard shortcut is authorship

## usage

- pairs with the [[bow]] in gift contexts: thank-you cards, packaging tape
- solo as a like-mark: stamping a favorite, marking a client win
- outlined hearts as bullet points, sparingly: see [[graphic elements]] for density rules

## rules

- never use the emoji heart in brand contexts: if it can be typed in a second, it can't be the brand
- hearts are accents: three per composition is a valentine, not a brand
