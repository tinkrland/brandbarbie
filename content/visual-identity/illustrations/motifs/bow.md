the bow is the brand's signature: the one shape that shows up until showing up is the meaning.

## anatomy

- loops are round, slightly uneven: a hand-tied bow, never a stencil
- two ribbon tails, one always a little longer: perfect symmetry reads corporate, and we are not that
- the knot is small and slightly off-center: charm lives in the imperfection

## usage

- big bows anchor compositions: one per layout, at hero scale
- small bows punctuate: the dot on the i, the period at the end (see [[motifs]])
- fill, outline, or cut out of a shape: never all three in one composition

## rules

- never stretch a bow to fill space; repeat it instead
- the bow doesn't spell anything and doesn't wear text: it is not a badge, the badge is the [[submark]]
