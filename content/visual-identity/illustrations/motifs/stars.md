the four-point sparkle is the brand's energy unit: small, tilted, and used only where delight is warranted.

## anatomy

- four points, never five: five-point stars are someone else's flag
- concave curves between the points: a sparkle, not a sheriff's badge
- always tilted 15 to 30 degrees off vertical: the jaunty angle is the personality

## usage

- twinkles around the [[the bow|bow]] and headlines: one to three per composition
- the accent shape in sticker sheets and dividers
- paired with the sugar dots for confetti moments: launches, sales, birthdays

## rules

- straight-up sparkles look like clip art: if it isn't jaunty, it isn't ours
- sparkles never carry meaning on their own: they decorate a real thing, they don't replace it
