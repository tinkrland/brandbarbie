icons are illustration's serious siblings: small, fast, unambiguous.

## the system

- 2px stroke on a 24px grid, round caps, round joins
- corner radius matches the type's roundness — soft, never sharp
- fill icons are solid single-color; no multi-color icons
- every icon must read at 16px or it doesn't ship

## sparkle exceptions

exactly three icons get a decorative star: favorite, new, and sparkle. no other icon may be cute — cuteness is rationed so it stays valuable.
