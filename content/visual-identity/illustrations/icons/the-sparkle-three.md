cuteness is rationed so it stays valuable. exactly three icons in the set earn a decorative star, and the list is closed.

## the three

- **favorite**: the heart icon, plus a small sparkle at its upper right
- **new**: the star icon itself, allowed to twinkle
- **sparkle**: the standalone four-point [[stars|star]], used for delight moments

## why a closed list

- if every icon is cute, none of them are: scarcity is the mechanism, see [[personality & values]]
- a fourth sparkled icon needs a brand review, not a designer's enthusiasm
- the sparkle sits at the icon's upper right, 8px offset, 60% of the icon's scale: same placement every time

see [[icons]] for the system these three live in.
