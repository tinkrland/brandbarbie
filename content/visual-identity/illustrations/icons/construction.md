icons are built on strict geometry so they feel like a family: same stroke, same grid, same corners.

## the geometry

- 24px grid, 2px stroke, round caps, round joins
- key shapes sit on the grid's 2px increments: an icon that misses the grid wobbles next to one that doesn't
- corner radius matches the type's roundness: soft, never sharp
- 2px minimum inner spacing between strokes: below that, the icon fuses into mush at small sizes

## the readability gate

- every icon must read at 16px or it doesn't ship: draw it, shrink it, squint at it
- one metaphor per icon: a heart plus a star plus a bow is a soup
- test on the cream background and on cocoa ink: icons live on both
