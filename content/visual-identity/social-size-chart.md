one reference for every canvas the brand touches. dimensions in pixels, safe zones respected.

## profile & avatars

| placement | size | notes |
|---|---|---|
| instagram profile | 320 × 320 | use the [[logomark]] or [[monogram]] |
| favicon | 512 × 512 master | see [[favicon]] |

## instagram

- feed square: 1080 × 1080. text min 36px
- feed portrait: 1080 × 1350. preferred; more screen real estate
- story / reels: 1080 × 1920. keep {hl:orange|250px clear top & bottom} for ui
- carousel: 1080 × 1350, first slide does the hooking

## other channels

- tiktok: 1080 × 1920 (same safe-zone logic)
- pinterest: 1000 × 1500 (2:3)
- x / twitter: 1600 × 900 header, 400 × 400 avatar
- linkedin: 1200 × 627 shared image, 1584 × 396 banner
- youtube: 1280 × 720 thumbnail, 2560 × 1440 banner

## guidelines

- type inside images ≥ 36px on full-bleed canvases
- logos respect [[clear space]] at every size
- see [[social media]] application page for grid planning
