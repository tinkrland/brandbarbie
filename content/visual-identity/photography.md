photography is the brand's proof of life. the look: **warm, glowing, slightly overexposed** — golden hour that follows you indoors.

## shot rules

- natural light or convincingly-faked natural light; no gray-studio flatness
- color grade leans warm-pink; skin always looks like skin, never like a filter
- shallow depth of field; the subject pops from a creamy background

## props

- pink anything: glassware, ribbon, blooms, cakes
- cream and paper textures; 70s glassware; bows (obviously — see [[motifs]])
- one electric accent per frame — a lime, a red shoe; the wink

## editing presets

- exposure +0.3 to +0.7, blacks lifted, saturation restrained
- grain pass at 6% (see [[textures]]) — photos and illustrations share the same skin
