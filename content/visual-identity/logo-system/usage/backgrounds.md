the logo adapts to what's behind it. four cases:

## on light backgrounds
full color, the default.

## on dark backgrounds
mono white or cream: never the brand pink, which loses contrast (see [[contrast & accessibility]]).

## on photography
place in the calmest corner; add a scrim if the image is busy. the [[wordmark]] tolerates photos best.

## on brand-color backgrounds
mono, in either the background's own ink or cream. never full-color-on-color: that's a classic entry in [[logo misuse]].
