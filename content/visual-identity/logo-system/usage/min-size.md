below a certain size, letterforms collapse. minimums exist to stop that.

## minimums

- [[primary logo]]: 180px wide on screen, 35mm in print
- [[secondary logo]]: 140px / 28mm
- [[logomark]] & [[monogram]]: 32px / 8mm
- [[favicon]]: 16px (its whole job)

## when you must go smaller

don't. switch to the smallest variation that passes, or drop the logo entirely. a missing logo is better than a broken one.
