clear space is the empty area around the logo where nothing else may enter.

## the rule

- clear space = the height of the mark's own “b” on every side
- nothing, type, images, other logos, enters the zone

## why it matters

crowding is the most common form of [[logo misuse]]. the logo reads as premium precisely because of the emptiness around it. treat clear space as part of the logo, not the absence of one.
