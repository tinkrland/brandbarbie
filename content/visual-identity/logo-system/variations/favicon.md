the favicon is the logo at its most compressed: 16-32px in a browser tab.

## spec

- use the [[logomark]] or [[monogram]], simplified to survive pixel grids
- 512 × 512 master, exported down
- test at 16px: if it muddies, remove detail until it doesn't

## rationale

the favicon is the first brand impression in a browser — a blurry tab is a small but real credibility leak. see [[social size chart]] for the avatar variant
