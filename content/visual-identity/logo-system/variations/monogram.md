the monogram is the brand's initials, designed as a single unit — the fastest possible signature.

## where it lives

- favicon (see [[favicon]])
- embossed details, garment tags, DM stickers
- anywhere the brand needs to whisper

## note

the monogram trades recognition for convenience — pair it with the full name nearby until recognition is established
