the stamp is the submark in rubber-stamp treatment: inked texture, slight rotation, instant warmth.

## where it lives

- packaging tape, kraft paper, thank-you cards
- promotional graphics that want a handmade wink

## rules

- the texture is part of the mark — don't clean it up
- one stamp per composition; it's a garnish, not the meal
- color: ink on paper only, never on screen... except on screen, ironically, sometimes. keep it tasteful
