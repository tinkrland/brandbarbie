the wordmark is the brand name set in letterforms that are recognizable enough to stand without the mark.

## where it lives

- footers, colophons, merch tags
- anywhere the [[monogram]] is too cryptic and the [[primary logo]] is too much

## care

- never stretch, condense, or set in another font — see [[logo misuse]]
- the wordmark pairs with photography better than the full lockup does
