the logomark is the symbol alone, no text. it's what shows up at avatar size.

## primary uses

- social profile pictures (see [[social size chart]])
- app icons, stamps, watermark corners
- whenever width is limited but square space exists

## rules

- it must survive at 32px: that's the real test of the mark
- on color backgrounds it flips to mono per [[backgrounds]]
