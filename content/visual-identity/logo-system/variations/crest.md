the crest is the maximal version: mark, wordmark, year, and ornament in full ceremonial arrangement.

## where it lives

- anniversaries, launch events, award entries
- packaging for flagship products
- the back of a business card when the front stays minimal

## rules

- rare on purpose: frequency would kill the ceremony
- the crest always anchors its composition; never place it in a corner
