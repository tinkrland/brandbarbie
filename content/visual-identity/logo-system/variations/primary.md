the primary logo is the default. when in doubt, use this one.

## anatomy

- the full lockup: mark + wordmark, horizontal orientation
- brand pink mark, wordmark set in the brand serif (see [[brand typography]])

## when to use it

- website header, business cards, invoice footer
- anywhere with at least ~180px of width (see [[minimum size]])

## when not to use it

- tiny or square placements → [[logomark]]
- busy or low-contrast backgrounds → [[backgrounds]] rules
- anything playful/stamp-like → [[stamp]]
