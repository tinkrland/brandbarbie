graphic elements are the bits between the big pieces: frames, stickers, dividers, and shapes that give layouts their bounce.

## the set

- **frames**: rounded, 24px radius; photos and cards live inside them
- **stickers**: motifs (see [[motifs]]) with a white keyline, deployed like actual stickers: on the edge of things, slightly rotated
- **dividers**: one of three: a rule, a sugar-dot row, or a single sparkle
- **shapes**: blobs and arches used as photo masks; the arch is the house shape

## rules

- rotation budget: one rotated element per layout, max 4°. beyond that it's a yard sale
- stickers never overlap type; they overlap photos and each other
- the arch mask appears at most once per layout
