the brand board is the whole identity on a single canvas: the elevator pitch of the visual system. it's what gets posted, pinned, and sent.

## what goes on it

- one primary logo (see [[primary logo]]) and one [[logomark]]
- the core palette: 3 to 5 swatches with hex codes (from [[palette library]])
- type pairing: one display, one body (see [[brand typography]])
- 2-3 texture or motif samples (see [[textures]], [[motifs]])
- one mood image that anchors the feeling

## rules

1. it must read at thumbnail size: the [[minimum size]] logic applies to boards too
2. hex codes on the board, {hl:blue|always}: a palette without codes is a vibe, not a system
3. nothing on the board that isn't in the book; the board is a summary, not a free-for-all

:::note
the board is the *output* of the visual identity section: build it from the pages here, don't design it first and retrofit.
:::
