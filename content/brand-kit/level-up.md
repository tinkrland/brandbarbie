the level-up kit is the brand as a full wardrobe: everything in this book, in files.

## what's in it

- **the full logo suite** — every [[primary logo|variation]] from the logo system, all formats (see [[file formats]])
- **illustrations** — the [[illustration style]] library: motifs, patterns, textures, characters
- **packaging** — the unboxing system from [[packaging & merch]], print-ready
- **the guidelines doc** — this book, exported as a pdf, hyperlinked

## the honest note

:::note
most businesses never need this tier, and that's fine — it exists for teams, licensing, and moments when the brand has equity to protect (see [[brand equity|glossary]]). depth is a response to scale, not a substitute for it.
:::
