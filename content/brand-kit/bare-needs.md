the bare needs kit is the brand's survival kit: small enough to use everywhere, complete enough to be consistent.

## what's in it

1. **one logo** — the [[primary logo]] (png on transparent, 2000px wide)
2. **one variation** — the [[logomark]] for square placements
3. **2-3 colors** — from the [[bubblegum core palette]], hex codes included
4. **1-2 fonts** — display + body (see [[brand typography]]), with free-license fallbacks if the real ones are paid
5. **profile pic** — the [[logomark]] on brand pink, 512px
6. **bio** — the house bios from [[social bios]]

## the promise

if a business applies *only this* consistently for a year, it will look more professional than 90% of its market. consistency beats completeness every time.
