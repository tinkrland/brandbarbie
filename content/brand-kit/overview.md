the brand kit answers the only question that matters when someone asks “send me the brand”: *what do you actually need right now?*

## the tiers

- [[bare needs]] — the minimum that works: one logo + a variation, 2-3 colors, 1-2 fonts, profile pic, bio
- [[nice to haves]] — the polish: submark, full palette, templates, motifs, brand board
- [[level up]] — the full suite: logo suite, illustrations, patterns, packaging, guidelines doc

## how to use this section

send people here instead of a 60mb zip. the tiers are honest: most daily needs are met by [[bare needs]] alone, and knowing that is the point.

see [[file formats]] before exporting anything — svg is not pdf is not png, and the differences are the whole game.
