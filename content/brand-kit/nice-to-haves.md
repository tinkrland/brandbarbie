the nice-to-haves are what take a consistent brand and make it feel *designed*.

## what's in it

- **the [[submark]]** — badges, seals, sticker moments
- **the full palette** — the whole [[palette library]] with ratios applied ([[color ratios]])
- **templates** — social templates from [[social media]], pinned and reusable
- **motifs** — the recurring symbols from [[motifs]], as pngs and svgs
- **the [[brand board]]** — the one-page summary, ready to post

## when to level up

add these once the [[bare needs]] are actually in daily use — polish on an unused foundation is just inventory.
