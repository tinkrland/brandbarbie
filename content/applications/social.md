social is where the brand lives daily, so the system is strictest here.

## the feed grid

- alternating rhythm: photo / graphic / photo / quote. a checkerboard of textures at thumbnail distance
- photo posts follow [[photography]]; graphic posts use one palette (see [[palette library]]) and one motif (see [[motifs]])
- carousels: first slide hooks, second delivers, the rest go deeper

## stories & reels

- 1080 × 1920, 250px safe margins top and bottom (see [[social size chart]])
- type enters bottom-third; brand stickers top-right
- covers: flat palette backgrounds, one motif per cover, no text

## highlights

- cover icons only, drawn from [[icons]], on brand-pink circles

see [[social bios]], [[calls to action]], and [[hashtags]] for the copy side.
