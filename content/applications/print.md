print is where texture gets physical. paper is a brand material here.

## stationery

- business cards: 3.5 × 2", cream uncoated 350gsm, logo per [[minimum size]]
- thank-you cards: a5 flat, one color inside — real handwriting beats perfect type
- stickers: the [[stamp]] and die-cut motifs on vinyl; hand them out like candy

## rules

- convert palettes to cmyk (see [[color codes]]); always proof before a run
- grain and riso textures print beautifully — use them (see [[textures]])
- never print the brand pink on uncoated stock without a proof; pink shifts the most of any color
