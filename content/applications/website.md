the website is the brand's home: all the personality, at its most organized.

## structure

- hero: one headline, one image, one button; the arch mask makes its appearance (see [[graphic elements]])
- sections alternate full-bleed photos with centered text blocks: see [[layout]]
- footer: the quietest place on the site; [[wordmark]], links, one sparkle

## rules

- typography follows [[website typography]] and [[type hierarchy]] exactly
- animations: 150ms ease-out; entrance motion is a gentle rise, not a circus
- every photo comes from [[photography]]; no gray-stock substitutions

:::note
write the hero in the brand voice: see [[voice & tone]] before writing a single headline.
:::
