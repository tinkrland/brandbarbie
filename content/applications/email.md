the inbox is a small, harsh canvas: 600px wide, dark mode lurking, a finger hovering over delete.

## newsletter

- header: 600 × 200, flat palette background, one motif — same header every issue; consistency is the feature
- body: 16px minimum, one column, no full-width images
- footer: a divider from [[graphic elements]], then plain text links

## signature

- name in the brand display face (as text, not an image), role in mono lowercase
- links as text, never a signature image strip

## rules

- subject lines: 4-7 words, lowercase, no emoji unless there's a bow involved
- test in dark mode: the brand pink must go darker gracefully (see [[palette library]])
