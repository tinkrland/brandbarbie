packaging is the brand's fourth dimension: time. the unboxing is a scene with a beat structure.

## the layers

1. **the box**: cream or kraft; logo per [[backgrounds]]; one arch window or nothing
2. **the tissue**: brand pink, sealed with the [[submark]] sticker
3. **the card**: thank-you note, handwriting first
4. **the product**: the payoff; photograph it for social (see [[social media]])

## merch

- totes: cream canvas, ink-only print, [[graphic elements]] in the bottom third
- apparel: the [[wordmark]] small on the chest or big on the back, never both
- pins & beads: motif-first (see [[motifs]])

:::tip
the unboxing should take 15 seconds to unfold. less, add a layer; more, remove one.
:::
