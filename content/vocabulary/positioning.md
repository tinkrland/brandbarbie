positioning is the sentence the brand is always answering.

## the positioning statement

> for creative small businesses who want a brand that feels *them, only sharper*, brandbarbie is a brand identity studio that turns what makes you distinct into a system you can actually use: because good design should make you more yourself, not less.

## the taglines

- **primary:** “be the brand they remember”. short, memorable, works anywhere
- **playful:** “bows before woes”. social, merch, stickers only (see [[social media]])
- **descriptive:** “brand identity, styled and systematized”. bios and bylines (see [[social bios]])

## the one-liner, by context

- elevator: “i make small brands look like the real deal.”
- invoice: “brand identity design”
- party: “i do brand. logos, palettes, the whole vibe.”
