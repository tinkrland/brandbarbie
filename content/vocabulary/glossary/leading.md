leading is the vertical space between lines: the measure of how comfortable a paragraph is to live in. pronounced "ledding", from the strips of lead old typesetters used.

## the settings

- print body copy: 1.4 to 1.5 times the type size
- web body copy: 1.6, see [[web body copy]]
- display headlines: 0.95 to 1.1, tight enough to read as one unit
- the longer the measure, the more leading: wide lines need air to find the next line

## the failure modes

- too tight: lines kiss, the paragraph turns into texture
- too loose: the eye lands between lines and rereads, the page turns into a list

## the rule

leading follows the [[type hierarchy]]: each rung has its setting, and ad-hoc leading is a per-paragraph mistake waiting to happen.
