x-height is the height of a lowercase x: the single best predictor of how a typeface feels at body size, and the fastest read on whether two faces can be paired.

## why it matters

- a tall x-height reads friendly and legible at small sizes: more of the letter is lowercase, rounder, warmer
- a short x-height reads elegant and editorial: more air, more preciousness
- brandbarbie leans tall: the warmth is the point

## the pairing check

- two faces with wildly different x-heights can't sit in the same paragraph: the [[display face]] over the [[body face]] is fine, side by side is not
- match x-heights when the faces share a composition, like axis labels next to body text

## the test

set a lowercase `xoxo` in both faces at the same size: if one xoxo looks like it belongs to a different book, the pairing is broken.
