brand equity is the accumulated trust a brand builds by being consistent: what makes a familiar logo worth more than a prettier new one.

## what it is

- recognition without explanation: the shorthand that lets one bow do a paragraph's work
- the permission to be minimal: a brand people know doesn't need to reintroduce itself
- a bank account: every consistent touchpoint deposits, every off-brand one withdraws

## what it isn't

- it isn't the logo: the logo is the handle, the equity is the weight
- it isn't instant: it compounds over years of showing up the same way (see [[brand strategy overview]])

## the discipline

- equity is why the misuse rules exist: every stretched logo is a small withdrawal, see [[logo misuse]]
- it's also why the brand evolves, not reinvents: a redesign spends equity to buy relevance, and the exchange rate is watched
