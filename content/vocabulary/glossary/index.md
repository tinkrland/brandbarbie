the glossary defines every term this book uses, so no page has to stop and explain itself.

## the terms (starter entries)

- **kerning** — space between two specific letters. the reason a logo looks off and you can't say why
- **submark** — the compact badge version of the logo. see [[submark]]
- **logomark** — the symbol, no text. see [[logomark]]
- **wordmark** — the name as the logo. see [[wordmark]]
- **brand equity** — the accumulated trust a brand can spend; what makes a familiar logo worth more than a prettier new one
- **brand board** — one-page visual summary. see [[brand board]]
- **clear space** — the no-go zone around a logo. see [[clear space]]
- **60/30/10** — the ratio rule for color. see [[color ratios]]
- **risograph** — a print process; digitally, the aesthetic of it. see [[textures]]
