kerning is the space between {hl:yellow|two specific letters}, adjusted pair by pair. it's the reason a logo can look slightly off with no obvious culprit.

## kerning vs tracking

- kerning fixes pairs: `AV`, `To`, `ry` are the usual suspects
- [[tracking]] adjusts the whole run: see the entry, the difference matters
- a headline with good tracking and bad kerning still looks broken

## where it matters most

- logos and wordmarks: every pair inspected, by eye, at final size (see [[wordmark]])
- display headlines at 48px and up: below that, kerning is invisible and tracking does the work
- never in body copy: the font's default kerning is right, trust it

## the test

squint, or turn it upside down: shapes without meaning reveal their spacing. if the gap between two letters reads differently from its neighbors, fix the pair, not the paragraph.
