tracking is the even, overall spacing of a whole run of letters: tightened for headlines, loosened for tiny caps labels.

## the rules of thumb

- big type gets tight: display headlines at 48px+ sit 1 to 2% tight
- small caps get loose: letter-spaced labels and eyebrows sit 5 to 10% open
- body copy gets neither: the font's default is the design

## tracking vs kerning

- tracking is the dial for the whole run; [[kerning]] is the fix for one pair
- tracking cannot fix a bad pair, and kerning cannot fix a bad run

## the brand's settings

- eyebrow labels (the small caps ones above headlines): 10% open, always
- the [[display face]] in headlines: 1 to 2% tight, checked with the squint test
- all-caps runs: 5% open minimum, because caps were drawn to be spaced
