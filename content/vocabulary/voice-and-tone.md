voice is the personality in sentences; tone is how it flexes by context. the traits come from [[personality & values]]: this page makes them speakable.

## the voice, spoken

- warm: *“you've got this. here's the exact file you need.”* {fold: (the anti-example) | never: *“the requested asset is attached.”*}
- candid: real numbers, real process, no mystique
- playful but precise: {hl:yellow|a joke never carries the meaning; the meaning carries a joke}
- encouraging: assume the reader can do it; hand them the tools ([[file formats]] is written this way on purpose)

## tone by context

| context | tone | example |
|---|---|---|
| launch announcement | sparkly, confident | “she's here.” |
| error / support | calm, no jokes | “let's fix this: here's what happened.” |
| pricing | plain, candid | the price, then why it's worth it, in that order |

## mechanics

- lowercase headlines are house style; sentence case for body
- contractions always; exclamation points: {hl:orange|one per page, budgeted}
- no corporate third person: the brand says *we*, the reader is {ul:wavy|you}
