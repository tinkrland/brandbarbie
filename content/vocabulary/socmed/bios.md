the bio is the brand at its smallest: name, promise, proof, in character.

## the house bios (starters)

- instagram (150 chars): “brand identity studio ✷ making small brands unforgettable ✷ currently obsessed with bows”
- x / twitter (160): “brand identity studio. logos, palettes, the whole vibe. bows before woes.”
- linkedin: “brandbarbie. brand identity studio. we turn what makes your business distinct into a visual system you can actually use.”

## rules

- one sparkle or bow emoji per bio; the budget is per-bio, not cumulative
- line breaks: name → jobs → personality. never more than three lines (see [[voice & tone]])
- the link goes to the [[website]] unless a launch is running
- tagline usage per [[positioning]]
