the bio is the brand at its smallest: name, promise, proof, in character, inside a character budget.

## the budgets

- [[bio 80]]: tiktok and the tight ones
- [[bio 150]]: instagram
- [[bio 160]]: x / twitter
- [[bio long]]: linkedin and facebook, where the bio becomes a paragraph

## the rules (every length)

- one sparkle or bow emoji per bio; the budget is per-bio, not cumulative
- line breaks: name → jobs → personality, never more than three lines (see [[voice & tone]])
- the link goes to the website unless a launch is running
- tagline usage per [[positioning]]
- the same brand in every length: the short versions aren't summaries, they're the same voice compressed
