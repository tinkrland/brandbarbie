long budgets (linkedin's about section, facebook's intro) turn the bio into a paragraph: the same voice, allowed to breathe.

## the house starter

- “brandbarbie. brand identity studio. we turn what makes your business distinct into a visual system you can actually use.”

## how to spend a paragraph

- open with the one-sentence version of the brand; it's the only line anyone reads for sure
- then one proof (what we've shipped) and one promise (what it's like to work with us)
- close with a plain invitation, not a cta banner: see [[calls to action]]
- still no jargon: length is not a license for “synergistic brand solutions”

see [[bios]] for the rules that apply at every length.
