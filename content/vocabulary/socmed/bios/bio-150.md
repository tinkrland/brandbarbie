instagram gives 150 characters and the line break, so the bio becomes a tiny poem: name, jobs, personality.

## the house starter

- “brand identity studio ✷ making small brands unforgettable ✷ currently obsessed with bows”

## how to spend 150

- line 1: what we do, the plain version
- line 2: the proof or the personality, pick one
- line 3: the arrow: where to click, what's new, or one obsession
- the link sticker below the bio carries the url; don't waste characters repeating it
