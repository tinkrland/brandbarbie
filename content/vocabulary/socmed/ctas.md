calls to action are the brand pointing. the pointing is confident; the hand is soft.

## the house ctas

- primary: **“book a call”**. the button everywhere on the [[website]]
- soft: “take a look →”. cards, secondary paths
- playful: “get the goodies”. shop, freebies, the [[brand kit overview]] download
- email: “read the letter”. never “click here”

## rules

- one primary cta per surface; everything else is a soft cta
- verbs first, lowercase, no “!”: {hl:yellow|the confidence is in the verb} (see [[voice & tone]])
- buttons get the brand pink, one weight, 24px padding: a cta is a brand element too (see [[graphic elements]])
