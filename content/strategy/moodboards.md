moodboards are where strategy becomes atmosphere. before picking a palette, collect the feeling.

## the brandbarbie mood, in five words

pink, glossy, soft, electric, nostalgic.

## what to collect

- **color moments** — candy storefronts, bubblegum, dusk light on skin, old barbie packaging
- **textures** — see [[textures]] for the official set (grain, gloss, glitter)
- **type sightings** — 70s serif signage, hand-lettered bakery windows
- **attitude** — studio portraits with one wink of chaos

## how this connects

every moodboard should be traceable: images → feeling → [[brand archetypes]]. if a board makes the brand feel aristocratic and cold, it belongs to a different project.

:::tip
keep two boards: *hero* (the brand at its most expressive) and *everyday* (the brand on a tuesday). the everyday board is the one the [[brand board]] should match.
:::
