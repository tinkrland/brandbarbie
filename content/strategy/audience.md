if everyone is the audience, no one is. this page defines who brandbarbie actually serves.

## ideal client

- creative small-business owners (solo to ~10 people) who sell something visual — jewelry, stationery, baked goods, photography, coaching
- they have taste but no time; they want the brand to feel *them*, only more polished
- they buy clarity: a system they can use themselves, not a 60-page pdf they'll never open

## personas

**maya, the maker.** runs a small product business from home. posts on instagram daily, uses canva, feels embarrassed by her own logo. needs: a [[brand board]] and [[bare needs]] kit she can apply without a designer.

**june, the studio owner.** small team, growing fast, inconsistent brand across channels. needs: [[type hierarchy]] and [[social size chart]] level systems so anyone on the team can post on-brand.

## who this is not for

- enterprise brand governance teams
- anyone looking for a rebrand in 48 hours
