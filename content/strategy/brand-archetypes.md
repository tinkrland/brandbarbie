archetypes are the characters a brand can play. brandbarbie is a blend of two.

## primary: the lover

warmth, connection, beauty, sensory pleasure. shows up as: soft palettes, rounded letterforms, photography of people close together, copy that says *you* more than *we*.

**watch out for:** the lover without structure becomes saccharine. that's why the secondary archetype matters.

## secondary: the creator

imagination, craft, self-expression. shows up as: process shots, custom illustration, templates that invite remixing, a visible love of making things.

## the blend

> beauty with craft: never beauty for its own sake, never craft without charm.

in practice: a [[brand board]] that feels warm and a [[type hierarchy]] that feels disciplined. both, always. when in doubt, check [[personality & values]].
