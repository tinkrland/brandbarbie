a brand is a promise kept consistently. this section is where that promise gets written down: before any logo, palette, or font exists.

## what lives here

- [[audience & ideal client]]: who this is for, and just as importantly, who it isn't
- [[brand archetypes]]: the character the brand plays
- [[personality & values]]: how it behaves under pressure
- [[moodboards]]: the feeling, collected

## the one-paragraph strategy

brandbarbie exists to make brand identity feel *playful and premium at once*. the visual world leans soft, pink, and expressive; the thinking underneath is rigorous. every design decision should hold both truths: {hl:sage|charming on the surface, deliberate underneath}.

## how to use this section

:::note
strategy pages are written to be re-read whenever a visual decision feels arbitrary. if a layout, color, or word choice can't be traced back to something in this folder, question it.
:::
