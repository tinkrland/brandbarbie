personality is strategy at human scale: the difference between a brand people recognize and one they tolerate.

## five traits

1. **warm**: speaks like a friend who happens to be an expert
2. **candid**: shows process, prices, and mistakes; no mystery pricing, no fake urgency
3. **playful**: pink, bows, and jokes, deployed with intent rather than as default
4. **precise**: every kerned letter, consistent spacing, receipts over vibes
5. **encouraging**: assumes the reader can do it themselves and hands them the tools

## values

- craft over trends
- clarity over cleverness
- generosity over gatekeeping
- consistency over novelty

## the line

the brand never punches down, never fakes scarcity, and never takes itself so seriously that a bow becomes impossible. if a piece of copy or design is clever but unkind, it's out.
